Emerging Filmmakers Project

We believe great stories come from creative collaboration. Our Emerging Filmmakers Project invites independent filmmakers, writers, actors, and cinematographers to work together on original short films. Participants will have the opportunity to share ideas, develop new skills, and bring unique creative visions to life while building connections within the local film community.

Looking for: Filmmakers, screenwriters, actors, and editors.
